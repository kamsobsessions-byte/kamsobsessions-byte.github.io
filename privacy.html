Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

YT Creator Tracker is a personal app used by one person, the owner of the connected YouTube channel, on her own computer.

What it reads from Google

With your permission, the app reads your YouTube channel details and YouTube Analytics reports (read-only access: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly). It never changes anything on your channel.

Where it is kept

The numbers are saved in your own tracker file on your own Mac and your own iCloud Drive. The Google sign-in key stays on your Mac. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared, or used for ads.

How to remove it

Press Disconnect in the app (Settings → Channel), or remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the tracker file at any time.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
