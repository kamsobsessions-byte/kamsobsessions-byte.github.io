YT Creator Tracker

YT Creator Tracker is a small personal app that one YouTube creator uses on her own Mac to plan videos, track the hours she works on them, and see her channel’s numbers.

When she connects her YouTube channel, the app reads that channel’s public details and YouTube Analytics numbers (views, subscribers, watch time, how viewers found each video). It only reads. It never posts, uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
